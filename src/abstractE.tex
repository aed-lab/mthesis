In recent years, there has been a lot of research on media science,
and the difference in temperature between faculty and students regarding
\LaTeX~samples has become a problem.
This can cause friction between faculty members and students, 
which can develop into a serious situation.
In this research, we first explored and identified the causes of such problems.
Furthermore, by applying the ``Age Difference Absorption Theory'' and
the ``Rudeness Skills'' method, we showed that the temperature difference can be rapidly converged.
We tested this method in two research projects and showed that the method is effective.

In the abstract, paragraph breaks are treated in the same way as in the main text.
In other words, forced line breaks and forced correction with full-width spaces are prohibited.
